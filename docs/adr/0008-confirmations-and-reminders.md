# ADR 0008 Confirmations, reminders and opt-out

Status: accepted, 29 October 2026.

T9 adds booking confirmation messages, reminder calls and reminder messages, quiet hours, a daily contact budget and opt-outs. Patient messaging and outbound calls need explicit owner authorization (AGENTS.md), and no messaging provider has been chosen, so everything here is built and tested against fakes and is off by default.

Decisions:

- Off by default, per clinic. `clinics.outbound_enabled` (migration 014) is false. Nothing is planned or sent for a clinic until an owner or admin enables it through `PATCH /v1/admin/clinic`, and each send checks it again. Enabling and disabling are audited separately (`clinic.outbound_enabled` / `clinic.outbound_disabled`). The worker contacts nobody without a port for the channel: `--messaging fake` is the only messaging option and, like the other fakes, is refused outside `_test`/`_dev` databases unless `APP_ENV=development`. Reminder calls use `--bolna` (fake or live). With `--bolna live`, enabling `outbound_enabled` places real calls, so it needs the owner's authorization first.
- No live messaging provider. `providers/messaging.py` defines `MessagingPort` (`send_message(to, body, reference)`, `get_message(reference)`) and its adapter obligations, plus `FakeMessaging`. Choosing a provider, recording its documentation and writing a contract-tested adapter is an owner action.
- One row per planned contact. `reminders` holds each confirmation, reminder call or reminder message of one appointment version. It records the channel, purpose, planned time, clinic-local `contact_day`, status, attempts, a short code, a stable `provider_reference` (`cvr_<id hex>`) and the provider execution or message id. It never holds a message body, number or name. `dedupe_key` (appointment, version, channel, purpose, lead minutes) is unique per clinic, so racing planners create a contact once and several kinds per appointment stay possible. Only the planner whose insert won adds the job (`notification.send` for messages, `reminder.call` for calls, payload `{reminder_id}`). One job per reminder is a unique index. Migration 014 narrows the old per-(appointment, kind, version) uniqueness to exclude these two kinds.
- Planned when the appointment is confirmed. The calendar.create handler's confirming transaction calls `reminders.on_confirmed`. A confirmation message is planned for now, and a reminder for `starts_at - reminder_lead_minutes` (default 24 h) per consented channel. Each needs an active consent for its purpose (`booking_confirmation`, `reminder_call`, `reminder_message`) and no matching opt-out. A reminder whose lead time has already passed is not planned. The confirmation covers it. Worker housekeeping (`reminders.schedule_due`) backfills reminders for confirmed appointments that enter the reminder window without one, for example after outbound was enabled or consent was granted later. The dedupe key makes the two planners safe together.
- Quiet hours and DST. `quiet_hours_start`/`end` (default 21:00-09:00, clinic-local, start after end wraps midnight, start = end refused) are evaluated on the clinic's IANA zone. A contact inside the window moves to the next allowed instant. The end wall time is resolved with both DST folds and the earliest allowed instant wins. An ambiguous end takes its first occurrence. An end that does not exist (spring forward) lands at most one hour after the transition. A contact is never moved later than 30 minutes before the appointment (`MIN_NOTICE`). If no allowed time fits, it is stored as `suppressed`/`quiet_hours`. The handler re-checks quiet hours at send time (settings may have changed) and defers its job without spending an attempt (`outbox.defer`).
- Daily budget. `daily_contact_budget` (default 200) caps contacts per clinic-local day. Planning counts contacts planned for the day. Sending counts contacts attempted that day, under a per-(clinic, day) advisory lock, which is the strict check. An over-budget contact is postponed to the next allowed time of the next day with room, within its window. Otherwise it is `suppressed`/`budget_exhausted`. It is never dropped silently. Confirmations count too.
- Re-check before contact. In one transaction a send locks the appointment (FOR SHARE), the budget day and the reminder row (lock order in `locks.py`). It re-checks the appointment status and version, the start time, expiry (12 h after the planned time), `outbound_enabled`, consent and opt-out. It then marks the row `sending` and checks its own fence while renewing its lease. Only then does it decrypt the phone and send with the connection idle. A stale version cancels the contact (`stale`). Policy outcomes are `suppressed` with a reason code.
- Cancellation and reschedule. `appointments.cancel_locked` (staff cancel, voice cancel and reschedule, review resolution) cancels the old version's unsent contacts in the same transaction. If that version has any contact, it also enqueues `reminder.stop`. The job sweeps unsent contacts again, waits while a contact is `sending`, and calls `BolnaPort.stop_scheduled_call` by execution id for each placed call without a stop outcome. A refused stop is recorded as `not_stoppable` unless a lookup shows the call was already stopped. A reschedule's new appointment gets its own contacts when it is confirmed.
- Calls are placed when due, not provider-scheduled. The worker calls `POST /call` without `scheduled_at` at the planned time, so consent, opt-out and cancellation are re-checked at the last moment. `stop_scheduled_call` therefore matters only for a call Bolna has queued but not yet started. The call carries `user_data` with the clinic name, appointment time, booking reference and our contact reference, and uses the clinic's `bolna_agent_id`. The agent's reminder wording is not designed here (owner action).
- Ambiguous outcomes. A timeout (or unclassified provider error) on a send marks the contact `unknown`. Before anything else, the next attempt reconciles a message through `get_message(provider_reference)`. If the message is found, the contact is sent. If it is not found, nothing was sent and the contact is attempted again under the same reference. If the lookup fails, the contact stays unknown and the attempt retries later. Bolna documents no lookup by client reference, and a timed-out `POST /call` returns no execution id. A call timeout therefore goes straight to `needs_review` (staff: `GET /v1/admin/reminders?status=needs_review`) and is never redialled. A `sending` row found by a new attempt (the worker died mid-send) is treated as `unknown`. Retryable errors return the row to `scheduled`. Permanent errors mark it `failed` and dead-letter the job. A dead contact job moves an unsettled row to `needs_review` and an unsent one to `failed`. Requeueing it does not re-contact.
- Opt-out requires verification. `POST /v1/bolna/tools/opt-out` applies only to the patient of a booking this call session verified (booking reference plus caller number, ADR 0006). It never applies to the caller's number alone. Caller ID is provider-asserted and spoofable, and opting out "this number" could silence another patient's reminders on a shared or spoofed phone. Unverified sessions get a speakable 403 that points to verification or staff. Staff can opt a patient out through `POST /v1/admin/appointments/{id}/opt-out` (owner, admin; audited with the appointment id). An opt-out (`contact_preferences`: purpose and channel, `all` matching everything) suppresses that patient's unsent contacts in the same transaction and blocks later planning and sending. Undoing an opt-out is not implemented.
- Webhook opt-out is a hook only. Bolna documents no opt-out field or disposition value, and the stored disposition codes are not documented vocabularies. `bolna.webhooks.disposition_opts_out` therefore always returns False, and nothing is inferred from free text or `extracted_data` (never stored).
- T7 review fold-in. When an unverified caller declines a purpose that has an active grant, the decline is still not applied. The reserve-slot `spoken` text now adds that changing an existing preference needs verification on the call or clinic staff.

Consequences: no live patient contact is possible without a messaging adapter (none exists), `--bolna live` and an owner or admin enabling outbound for the clinic. Outbound reminder executions create no call session, so their Bolna webhooks are kept as `unknown_execution` and their final call status is not tracked yet. During an outbound call `from_number` is likely the clinic's line, so the opt-out tool cannot verify the callee and refuses. That is safe, but callees who say "stop" on a reminder call must be handled by staff until a contract-tested mechanism exists. Budget counts are per clinic-local day and assume the clinic timezone does not change between planning and sending.
